# Told You demo flow

1. Open the landing page: same budget, same timer, better trader wins.
2. Creator opens a five-minute round with a dedicated Shannon wallet that has at least 100 tUSDC.
3. Copy the round link and open it with a different funded wallet.
4. The rival joins; the shared timer begins only after both wallets press Ready.
5. Each player records a simulated buy or sell action for BTC/ETH Event Contract positions. Explain that the market and oracle data are live, while the demo does not submit a wallet order.
6. Show the round board: recorded demo fills, remaining buy cap, open positions, common live marks, and PnL.
7. At the deadline, explain that the score is recorded round cash plus marked open positions, not unrelated wallet balances.

Show the demo fixture without wallet actions at:

`/arena?demo=1&round=round-demo`
