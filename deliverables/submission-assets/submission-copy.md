# Told You

## One-line description

Told You turns DreamDEX Event Contracts into a five-minute, head-to-head trading duel.

## Vision

Event Contract trading is usually a solo experience: a user picks an outcome, buys a contract, and waits. Told You adds the social loop. Two players verify the same tUSDC round budget, press Ready, and trade independently against the same five-minute clock. The winner is the trader with the better marked PnL.

Told You reads live BTC and ETH index prices from the Somnia oracle feed and UP/DOWN outcome prices from DreamDEX market data. It deliberately shows these as separate signals, so traders can distinguish the underlying asset price from the probability reflected in an Event Contract.

The current hackathon build uses safe simulated execution at the displayed mark. It integrates real DreamDEX market and Somnia oracle reads while avoiding unverified wallet-order claims. The product also includes open-position tracking, market and limit controls, rivalry records, and a season Rating model.
